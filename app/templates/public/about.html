About Us

Wellness, nourishment, and movement in one beautiful place.

Sana Sana was created to blend two everyday essentials into one experience: a feel-good cafe and a grounded pilates studio. Our biggest goal is to make our products and services accessible to everyone, so they can live a healthy life no matter where they are in their journey.

Our story

What started as a simple idea — a space where people could nourish themselves and move with intention — became a concept that combines fresh food, thoughtful routines, and a welcoming studio atmosphere.

Our philosophy

We center every decision around balance: fresh ingredients, mindful movement, warm hospitality, and a lifestyle that feels sustainable rather than rigid.

Healing with purpose

Wellness should meet you where you are.

If getting upstairs to our farmstand is difficult because of a disability or mobility barrier, send us a message. We will personally deliver your order at no charge.

No one should feel limited in access to health, wellness, or something made with love.

What makes us different

  • Fresh cafe offerings that support daily energy
  • A pilates studio experience rooted in community and confidence
